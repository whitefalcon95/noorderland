THE GREAT BREACH & THE FALL OF THE POLDERS

When the global collapse hit in 2028, the automated water management systems of the Netherlands ran unmaintained for 72 hours. Cyber warfare triggered the emergency floodgates of the Delta Works, breaching the major dikes and unleashing the North Sea into the low-lying provinces.

What remains today is Noorderland: a drowned realm where survivors navigate flooded polder towns using improvised watercraft, brave contaminated military waterworks bunkers, and fight for control over high-ground military outposts in the dense pine forests of the Veluwe.

"The dikes didn't just keep the sea out... they kept our secrets in. Now the water controls who lives and who drowns."
— Commander V. van Rijn, Dutch Defense Remnant
